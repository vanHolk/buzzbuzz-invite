Walk **10,000 steps** sometime during Halloween week. At least **one of those walks happens after sunset**. That is the whole rulebook.

You can split the steps across the day. The night walk is the part that makes it Halloween. Porch lights, cold air, and your own street looking slightly like a film set.

## How to play it

- **Before dark:** bank 4,000–6,000 steps, so the night walk is a finale and not a march.
- **After dark:** go out for the rest. Twenty to forty minutes is enough if you already started. Ideally with at least one other person.
- **Proof:** one photo from the night walk. A lit window, a plastic skeleton, your own shadow. No need to photograph strangers.
- **Finish line:** everyone who hits 10,000 and sends a night photo is done. It is not a race unless your group wants a race.

If 10,000 steps sounds abstract, it is usually [90–120 minutes of walking](/guides/how-long-to-walk-10000-steps) in total. The spooky portion can be the short one.

:::bee
The spooky part is optional. The together part is not. A night walk is better with a friend, a lit street, and permission to turn around when you are cold.
:::

## Keep the night walk easy

- Stick to streets you know, with lights and other people around.
- Tell someone the loop before you leave, even if they are on it with you.
- Wear something a driver can see. A light jacket beats a tasteful all-black outfit.
- Headphones low, or one ear free.
- If a block feels wrong, pick another block. The challenge does not require bravery.

Cold is the usual boss fight, not the dark. Pockets, a warm drink at the end, and a route that passes somewhere open. You are collecting steps, not surviving a forest.

## If the hive wants extra credit

Give the week a tiny theme. Everyone photographs the same color. Orange is the obvious one. Purple is the better one. In BuzzBuzz that kind of prompt is a Color Hunt, but any group chat can borrow it for seven days.

When the pumpkins come down, keep the part that was never seasonal: [a walking streak with friends](/guides/start-a-walking-streak-with-friends). Same group. Same check-in. No costume required.
