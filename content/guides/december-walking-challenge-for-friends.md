You and a few friends can share one step total this December. For a 260,000-step goal over 24 days, two walkers need about 5,417 steps each a day. Ten walkers need about 1,084. That number is a share of a pooled total, not a personal pass or fail. Miss a day, and the rest of the hive can still cover it.

## Split 260,000 steps across 24 days

The hive's combined target is just over 10,833 steps a day. The table rounds each person's daily share up, so if everyone hits their number the group still clears 260,000.

| Friends in the hive | Daily share per person |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 5,417 |
| 3 | 3,612 |
| 4 | 2,709 |
| 5 | 2,167 |
| 6 | 1,806 |
| 7 | 1,548 |
| 8 | 1,355 |
| 9 | 1,204 |
| 10 | 1,084 |

A hive of one does not get this mission. Two is the minimum. A hive of eight looks easy on paper, at 1,355 steps a day, but only if those eight actually walk. A reliable four may finish faster than a quiet ten.

The shares assume an even split. If one friend walks to work and another spends the day at a desk, let the heavier walker carry more. The goal is the hive total. For groups spread across time zones, keep each person's day on their own local clock, as in the [guide to walking with friends who live far away](/guides/walk-with-friends-who-live-far-away).

At a steady 100 steps a minute, the pace used in [how many steps is a 30-minute walk](/guides/how-many-steps-is-a-30-minute-walk), 5,417 steps is about 54 minutes of walking, which can be an errand and an evening loop. 1,084 steps is about 11 minutes. Treat those times as planning estimates, not a medical target.

Only steps from December 1 through 24 count toward the 260,000. A walk on the 25th is a nice walk. It does not go on this total.

## Check the halfway mark, then adjust

On day 12 the hive is halfway through the 24 days, so 130,000 steps is an even pace. If you're behind, divide what's left by the days remaining, then by the number of walkers.

Say four friends have 90,000 steps after 12 days. They have 170,000 steps and 12 days left, which is about 3,542 steps each a day, up from 2,709. That's still a walk most people can fit around work, and one longer weekend loop can take pressure off a travel day.

December will eat a few days. Parties, airports, and short afternoons all count as reasons the even split slips, not as reasons to quit. A 20-minute indoor loop still counts; [indoor steps](/guides/too-cold-to-walk-indoor-steps) are the same steps. If you walk after dark, the [guide to walking after the clocks go back](/guides/walking-after-the-clocks-go-back) covers light and a short minimum. Carry the phone or watch that is doing the counting, or a walk around the house will not show up.

Agree on a simple check-in, such as one photo from the walk, so the group sees more than a number. If you still need a group, start with [what a hive is](/guides/what-is-a-hive) and the [walking streak playbook](/guides/start-a-walking-streak-with-friends).
