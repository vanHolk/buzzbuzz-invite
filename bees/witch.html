Witch Bee

The moon on the hat is always right. The step count is always higher.

Character file

Classification Halloween bee. The license is written in honey.
Active hours Whenever the moon is up. A porch light has been counted.
Step style Unhurried, then suddenly across town. The bell does not jingle. It decides.
Diet Honey left out overnight. She calls this finishing.
Current status In the Honey Shop for Halloween.

Field notes

Witch Bee took the corner opposite Vampire Bee's coffin and changed the curtains. He changed them back. They have not discussed it. The curtains are currently half and half, which both of them are counting as a win.

The crescent on the hat matches the moon. The hive watched it for a week, got bored, then checked one more night out of spite. It matched. She said "obviously" and would not explain the other six hats in the closet.

The gold bell is not jewelry. Once means yes. Twice means walk. It stays quiet if anyone asks whether the fangs are real. She says she does not have fangs. The bell has no comment.

Her step log says errands. The errands are always a round ten thousand, and they always end at a flower that was not on that corner yesterday. She calls it gardening. The garden was a sidewalk.

Witch Bee is in the BuzzBuzz Honey Shop for Halloween. If the bell rings twice, you are already late.

From the bell

Curtain treaty

Half blackout velvet, half whatever was already there. Talks are over.

Spare hats

Six. She says they are for the moon. The moon has not issued a dress code.

The bell's name

It has one. Saying it makes the bell ring, which she accepts as consent.

Sidewalk garden

New flowers at exactly 10,000 steps. Botany opened a file. She did not.
