Vampire Bee

Sleeps all day. Somehow still hits 10k before midnight.

Character file

Classification Halloween bee. Possibly immortal. Definitely dramatic.
Active hours Sunset to whenever everyone else gives up on the leaderboard.
Step style Suspiciously elegant. Cape flapping at full speed.
Diet Honey. Allegedly.
Current status Coming to the Honey Shop this October.

Case notes

Nobody knows exactly when Vampire Bee moved into the hive. One evening there was an empty corner. The next morning there was a tiny coffin, a cape on the coat hook, and somebody had changed the curtains to blackout velvet.

He insists he does not sleep all day. He is "resting strategically." This strategy usually ends around sunset, when he appears with perfect hair and zero recorded steps, then somehow climbs the leaderboard before midnight.

The fangs look alarming. They mostly open honey packets.

Nobody has actually seen Vampire Bee drink blood. Nobody has seen him eat garlic either. The hive has agreed not to investigate further because, frankly, he is very polite and his cape looks expensive.

Vampire Bee joins the BuzzBuzz Honey Shop for Halloween this October. Night walks encouraged. Neck protection optional.
