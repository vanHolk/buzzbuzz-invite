Suho

Scholar mode. Songpyeon clause: always.

Official bee file

Vibe Bows first. Snacks first. Gat still on.
Natural habitat Chuseok courtyards, songpyeon trays, anywhere a gat looks official.
Step style A bow's pace. Accelerates the moment rice cakes appear.
Honey order Songpyeon first. Honey second. The order is negotiable.

The lore

Suho put on his best gat and durumagi for Chuseok — Korea's harvest festival, the one with family bows and a very full moon — and has been practicing his most dignified greeting ever since. Official position: greet first, snack second. Unless the snack is songpyeon. Then the order is under review.

The gat is not a fashion statement. It is a responsibility. The beaded chin strap is holding. He checked. The tasseled sash is also holding. He did not check as carefully, because checking ornaments is not, in his words, "scholarly."

On low-step days he wants the gat off and considers this a private matter. On high-step days he declares the songpyeon has arrived and becomes, in his words, "unable to resist. Obviously." Dignity is restored after the third rice cake. Approximately.

Bee facts

Bow policy

Bows first. Snacks first. He can hold both thoughts.

Gat report

Still on. Heavy on low-step days. Respected on high-step days.

Songpyeon clause

Dignified until someone brings out the rice cakes. Then: cannot resist.

Harvest buddy

You are his favorite walking buddy. He bowed when he said it.
