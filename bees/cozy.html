Cozy Bee

The days are getting shorter. Her calendar is getting longer.

Character file

Classification November bee. Organizer, opinion-haver, plan-maker.
Active hours Whenever someone might be home alone with nothing on.
Step style Quick and talking the whole way. She stops at every bookshop window to check whether the staff picks have improved. They haven't.
Diet Diner toast and coffee, plus whatever the pie of the week is, as long as she approves of it.
Current status In the Honey Shop for November.

Case notes

Cozy Bee has decided the hive won't have a lonely November, and she didn't ask anyone before deciding. By the first frost she had a book club, a soup swap, and a Thursday trivia night on the calendar. She'd also booked a Friday for "lantern stuff," which she still hasn't explained.

She knows everyone's business, and she knows it early. She knew Goblin Bee had switched barbers before Goblin Bee's barber knew. She has notes on the harvest fair parking plan and on the font on the fair flyer. Most of them are correct, and none of them were requested.

She has had the same window seat at the corner diner every morning since she arrived. The owner says he only saves it so she'll stop rearranging the other tables. One Tuesday, Boram's mug was still on its hook for the third day running. Cozy Bee ordered a second slice of pie to go, didn't say who it was for, and took the long way home past Boram's door.

Her mother knitted the scarf the winter Cozy Bee left home. It's a little too long because her mother kept going while they talked on the phone. Cozy Bee will tell you it's uneven. She has never once let anyone else fix it.

Cozy Bee is in the BuzzBuzz Honey Shop for November. If you're free Thursday, you're not anymore.

Notes from the town meeting

Hat contents

Three paperbacks and a spare event schedule. The pom-pom is mostly structural.

Town meetings

Six. She has never missed one, and she has opinions on items not yet on the agenda.

The diner owner

"That seat's not yours." It has been hers every morning for a year.

Signup sheets

She has already put your name on one, and you'll find out which.
