Boram

Full moon. Full hanbok. Full heart.

Official bee file

Vibe Three hours on the hair. Still twirling.
Natural habitat Moon-viewing nights, songpyeon trays, anywhere a chima can spin.
Step style Twirl. The skirt sets the pace, and the skirt came to be seen.
Honey order One plate of moonlight. Songpyeon on the side. No notes.

The lore

Boram pinned her hair up for Chuseok — Korea's harvest festival, the big family-and-full-moon one — and has not sat still since. Official position: full moon, full hanbok, full heart. The gold pin is doing a lot of work. She is aware. She will not apologize.

Three hours on the hair. That is not a complaint. That is a status report. On moon-viewing night she twirls so the coral chima catches the light, then checks if you noticed. You noticed. She already knew.

On low-step days the braids feel heavy and she files it as a tragedy. On high-step days she asks, very casually, "Don't I look pretty today?" as if the harvest moon itself did not already vote yes. The hive voted yes. The songpyeon voted yes. Case closed.
