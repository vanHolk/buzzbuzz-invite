Michael Holker

Michael is the founder of BuzzBuzz and a Senior Product Designer with over 10 years of experience making technology a little more human.

He designed, built and shipped BuzzBuzz for iOS and Android. Because apparently, getting people to go outside requires building an entire digital world full of bees.

He believes walking should be enjoyable, technology shouldn't get in the way, and not everything in life needs a productivity metric. Except maybe steps. He's a little biased on that one.
